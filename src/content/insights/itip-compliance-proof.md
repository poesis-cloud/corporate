# Continuous IT compliance

Regulatory compliance is usually reconstructed before an audit. Teams collect evidence from scattered systems, map it to clauses, and hope the gaps are small enough to explain away.

This case study shows how an IT organization sourced NIS2 and DORA into GSM, attached the resulting Directives and Norms to their actual architecture, and used the ITIP compliance dashboard to watch coverage continuously — not as a spreadsheet, but as a live model.
