# Continuous IT compliance

Regulatory compliance is usually reconstructed before an audit. Teams collect evidence from scattered systems, map it to clauses, and hope the gaps are small enough to explain away.

This is a **reference model**, not a customer deployment. It shows how an IT organization would source NIS2 and DORA into GSM, attach the resulting Directives and Norms to its actual architecture, and read compliance coverage continuously from the governed model rather than from a spreadsheet. The sourced framework definitions it builds on are published; the integrated ITIP compliance dashboard is still in development.
